Every event shows its own refund policy on the event page and on your ticket. That policy is set by the organizer.

Free events

Cancel any time before the event from My tickets. Your seat goes to someone on the waitlist.

Paid events

  • If the policy allows a refund, it goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
  • If an event is cancelled by the organizer, you get a full refund of the ticket price.
  • Some events allow a free transfer to a friend instead of a refund. The event page says so.

Need help?

Write to contact@eventouse.com with your booking reference.
