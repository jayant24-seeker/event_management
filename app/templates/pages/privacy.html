{{ site_name }} is run by N Square Digital Solutions ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the website, why, and your choices. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: your mobile number, and the name and email you add.
  • Bookings: the attendee details an organizer asks for (for example name, age, gender, T-shirt size, emergency contact) and what you booked.
  • Payments: handled by our payment partner. We never see or store your card or UPI PIN.
  • Usage: basic logs (pages visited, device type) to keep the site secure and working.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in with a one-time code and to issue your tickets.
  • To share attendee details with the organizer of the event you booked, who needs them to run the event.
  • To send you messages about your bookings, and waitlist alerts you asked for.

We don't sell your data, and we don't send marketing messages without your consent.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for 8 years for tax and accounting purposes. Other data is deleted when you delete your account.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, by writing to contact@eventouse.com. We reply within 30 days.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. Saved events for guests stay in your own browser.
